# Competitor landscape: AI how-to, product demo and explainer video tools

Read 2026-11-27.
All claims come from each vendor's own pricing or home page unless marked "third party".
No third-party reviews were used.
Prices are list prices seen that day, many with Black Friday discounts; treat them as a snapshot.
Nothing was signed up for, signed into or downloaded.

## Summary

The market splits three ways: capture tools (Scribe, Tango, Guidde, Trupeer, Clueso, Supademo, Arcade) that turn a recording into guides or video, avatar presenter tools (Synthesia, HeyGen, Colossyan, Vyond, Pictory, invideo) that turn a script or document into video, and editors (Descript, Loom) that fix recordings.
Showhow's no-recording, code-derived approach is rare: only the capture tools' newest "agent records for you" modes (Clueso, Supademo) and Arcade's app connectors come close.
showhowai.com is a near twin of the new `explainer` mode (script or topic in, one visual per sentence, 16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1, brand kit, comments at the second, $1 trial, $9 first video, $19-79 a month); its pitch is "animated in code" with a script you approve and preview stills before render.
Nearly every product now ships an MCP server or "make videos from Claude" path (Arcade, Clueso, Supademo, Descript, Pictory, Synthesia, HeyGen, Scribe, Tango), so showhow's agent-native angle is table stakes in distribution, not a moat.
The biggest gaps are reusable style templates (Clueso Clueprints), approve-before-render preview stills, multi-language dubbing and translation, interactive output (quizzes, branching, SCORM), a brand kit, and auto-updating a video when the product changes.

### showhowai.com
Closest to the new `explainer` mode.
Flow: you approve a script, then a scene plan, then preview stills in your colours, then it renders; changes to words change only that scene.
It outputs 9:16, 16:9 and 1:1 "laid out for its frame, never cropped", caption files, stock or own voice, its own licensed score, and an originality check against your recent videos.
Unmarked gaps are labelled `[FILL]` in the script rather than invented.
Distribution is a $1 trial and an iPhone app; no avatars.

### Guidde
Capture a workflow, get a narrated video with AI voice and a brand kit.
Business adds PDF/PPT/Docs to video, interactive demos and analytics; Enterprise adds translation and PII redaction.
Broadcast pushes the video into the apps people use; Ask answers questions from the video library.
Free tier caps at 25 videos.

### Scribe
Documentation first, video second.
Autocapture turns work already done into guides automatically.
Pro adds branding, screenshot redaction and PDF/HTML/Markdown export; Enterprise adds auto-redaction, translation, approval workflows and an MCP server.
Pitches guides as context for AI agents.

### Tango
Click-and-talk capture into a guide with screenshots.
Pro adds branded exports, voice and video embeds, PII blur, viewer insights.
Enterprise adds Guide Me (live in-app walkthrough), Pins, automations, translation and an MCP connector.

### Arcade
Now an "AI video playground for marketing teams": record with the extension (smart zooms and cursor), or build from Notion, Figma or Linear, or from Claude/ChatGPT via MCP.
Selling points are on-brand output, memory of your tone and choices, and "edit anything by asking AI".
Credits are charged per AI action, with a receipt after each request; manual edits are free.
Interactive demos are a separate plan.

### Supademo
Interactive step demos, 4K video, HTML and sandbox demos.
New agent flow: connect the app, GitHub and Linear once; an agent records in the cloud and updates demos after a release, about 10 minutes per demo.
Also a voice AI "Demo Agent" in 50+ languages that runs demo calls, priced per reply.
Scale adds branching, variables, tracking links and analytics.

### Loom
Recording plus AI polish: auto title, summary, chapters, filler word and silence removal, video-to-doc and video-to-ticket.
"Record for agents" turns a screen walkthrough into a structured prompt or Jira item.
Not a production tool; relevant for the written-companion and chapters ideas.

### Synthesia
Avatar platform with doc, PPT or URL to video, screen recorder, motion graphics, dubbing and translation (140+ languages with a glossary).
Publishes as link, embed or SCORM; edits propagate to links and LMS copies.
Analytics (views, clicks, watch-through by version and language), approval before publish, roleplay sessions, API and MCP.
Credit-based pricing.

### HeyGen
Avatar 6 (photo to talking avatar, 175+ languages with lip-sync), Video Agent (prompt to share-ready video), voice clone, 4K on Pro, MCP.
Relevant to showhow mostly for localisation and one-prompt entry.

### Colossyan
Source (PDF, deck, URL, video) to scene-by-scene plan that you review, then edit, then translate into 120+ languages with lip-sync.
Quizzes, buttons and branching in the same editor, with scores sent to an LMS via SCORM.
Free AI tools (learning objectives, assessments, dubbing) as lead magnets.

### Vyond
Animated characters plus AI avatars, Vyond Go (text/doc/script/URL to video), translation into 95 languages, brand management suite on Enterprise and Agency.
Priced per user, highest in the set, with duration caps (25 to 90 minutes by plan).
Page read in the built-in browser after Cloudflare blocked curl.

### Descript
Edit video by editing a transcript, plus the Underlord assistant, Studio Sound, filler word removal, Brand Studio templates and guardrails, API and MCP.
Business adds dubbing in 30+ languages and custom avatars.

### Pictory
Script, article or URL to stock-footage video, now with an AI video agent and a scene editor.
Enterprise adds AI chapters, clickable CTAs, SCORM, quizzes and knowledge checks.
Pictory MCP on all plans.

### invideo
Credit-based prompt-to-video with frontier video models and "Agent Two" tiers.
Timeline editor and multiplayer editing.
Not a how-to tool; relevant as the generative-footage end of the market.

### Clueso
Closest feature set to showhow among capture tools.
Inputs: recording, deck, PDF, prompt, or a browser agent that navigates your product for you.
Clueprints capture a video's story, look, motion and sound as a reusable standard applied to later videos.
Also: documentation from the same video, translation glossary and rules, magic blur, quizzes, branching, lead forms, SCORM export, MCP.

### Trupeer
Recording to polished video plus written guide.
Auto annotations, rewrite with AI, brand kit with logos, backgrounds and music, glossary and pronunciations (Enterprise).
Translation in 100+ languages in bulk, knowledge base with AI search, reviewer and approval workflow, content health checks, smart blur.

## Top 8 ideas for showhow, by value over effort

1. Preview stills plus an explicit "approve script and plan" stop before render.
   Cheapest trust win, copies showhowai.com and Colossyan, and a wrong frame costs minutes to fix before and hours after.
2. Clueprint-style saved style packs.
   Extend `verdicts.md` and `edits.md` into a named, reusable pack (story shape, visuals, motion, sound) applied with `--pack <name>`; Clueso's headline feature and it fits the file-based house style.
3. One brand kit input (logo, colours, fonts, music, voice) read from a file or extracted from the repo, used by howto, update and explainer alike.
   Every competitor has it; Low effort because theme extraction already exists.
4. Glossary and pronunciation file per project.
   Clueso, Trupeer and Synthesia all lead with it; showhow already respells TTS input, so persist those fixes and reuse them.
5. "Refresh this video" mode: diff the code since the last render, patch only the changed scenes, re-render.
   Matches Supademo's stale-demo agents and Synthesia's edit-propagation, and git history makes it uniquely cheap here.
6. Multi-aspect output in a single run for every mode, not only `explainer`.
   Already planned for explainer; showhowai.com sells it as a core point.
7. Localisation pass: translate script, captions and on-screen text, pick a Kokoro or other local voice per language, keep glossary rules.
   Most requested enterprise feature across the set; scope to captions plus voice where a model exists.
8. Quiz and chapter-aware export: an interactive HTML wrapper with end-of-video questions and a SCORM zip.
   Colossyan, Clueso, Synthesia and Pictory charge for it; medium effort, opens training buyers, lower priority than 1-7.

Not recommended: avatars, voice cloning, analytics and in-app live guidance.
They are expensive, off-brand for a no-recording local pipeline, and need hosted infrastructure.
